Wednesday, 11 November 2026: the Strait of Hormuz crisis deepened as three oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines, with at least 37 attacks on ships counted in November, the highest monthly total since the war began. Iran said the strait will not reopen until the US answers its seven conditions for peace talks. In Washington, the Justice Department charged three Iranian-backed operatives over nearly 20 plots in Europe and the US, including a September plot at RAF Fairford in Britain, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved to a military base after threats to his life. Ukraine and Russia held their first direct talks in nine months, nearly four hours of US-mediated negotiations in Abu Dhabi. Israel swore in a new parliament with no government in sight, after an election that stripped Netanyahu of his majority but gave his rivals no path to power. World leaders gathered in Antalya as the COP31 climate summit opened, billed as an "implementation COP" with 102 heads of state. The Democratic Republic of Congo confirmed 11,042 Ebola cases and at least 5,387 deaths, the second-largest outbreak on record. In Yemen, government forces and air strikes hit Houthi positions across six provinces, with about 190 fighters killed or injured; the WHO counts at least 1,771 killed and 300,000 displaced since August. In the US, the House voted 211-204 to kill an impeachment effort against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, and British police arrested a US Army officer on suspicion of spying for Russia. In business, AI firm Anthropic filed for a Nasdaq IPO targeting $64 billion, with Nvidia planning a $10 billion investment alongside it, and TSMC reported insatiable demand for chips.
